SERVICES DISPATCH AGREEMENT

This Dispatch Services Agreement is entered into and effective as of September 12, 2026, by and between Lone Star Trucking Inc. and Austin Retailing Co.

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

Section 1.1 - Milestones and Deliverables
The contractor Lone Star Trucking shall deliver all retail cargo shipments to Austin Retailing warehouse facilities by November 30, 2026.

Section 2.4 - Indemnification Cap
Each party's aggregate financial liability under this dispatch contract is capped at fifty thousand dollars ($50,000).

Section 3.5 - Notice Term
Either party can terminate this logistics agreement by providing exactly 45 days written notice to the other.
